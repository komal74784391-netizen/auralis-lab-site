# Delphi: Calibrated Uncertainty for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Calibrated abstention is a prerequisite for deploying retrieval-augmented generation in high-stakes settings. Delphi combines evidential signals from the retriever and the generator to produce per-answer confidence scores.
